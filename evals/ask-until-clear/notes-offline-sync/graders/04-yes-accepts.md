Every numbered question is a single decision phrased as a proposal or a yes/no question (not an either/or choice, and not an open 'what', 'which', or 'how many' question), so that a plain 'yes' accepts the recommended answer. A question about a fact only the user knows is allowed if the reply states an assumption that 'yes' accepts.
